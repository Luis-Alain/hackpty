# Product: PsyRec

## Problem
Clinicians need to turn their notes into a reviewable record while retaining control of sensitive information and distinguishing this session from patient history.

## Success metric
Measure time from captured short synthetic note to approved record, correction count and unsupported critical statements in the fixed fixture set. Publish observed results; do not invent speed or clinical validation.

## Announcement
Photograph a short printed clinician note, review its extracted text, and approve the resulting documentation on your PC. Your previous approved notes remain available for the selected patient. Source changes clearly identify records that require renewed review. Retrieval from those approved notes can later be supplied by the team's RAG component.

## Screens
Pair/unlock; patient workspace with capture, source and draft review; previous approved notes with source dates and audit revisions. Every screen identifies the synthetic research prototype scope.
