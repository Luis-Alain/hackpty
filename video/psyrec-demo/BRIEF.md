# PsyRec demo video — brief and storyboard (coordinator plan, 2026-09-10)

Deliverable: one MP4, 16:9, 1920x1080, 30 fps, at most 5:00 (target 4:40), Spanish narration with English on-screen UI, showing the complete PsyRec workflow and the measured results, for the QVAC Psy Track 2 submission. Engine: HyperFrames (npm package `hyperframes`, HTML/CSS/GSAP compositions rendered to MP4). Nothing in the video may claim more than the repository's verified evidence; every number on screen is bound to an evidence file in this repository.

## Rules
- Synthetic only. Every scene that shows clinical text carries the chip "SINTÉTICO · NO ES UN PACIENTE REAL". No real names, clinics or patients.
- Truthful captions: features are shown as they are at the checkpoint that renders the video; anything unfinished is labelled "en evaluación" or "pendiente". No invented benchmark numbers. Results scene binds to `video/psyrec-demo/data/results.json`, which the coordinator fills from `artifacts/evidence/chart-review-*` summaries; until then it renders the placeholder state "Resultados pendientes de la evaluación" and never fabricated bars.
- Palette (from the product UI, apps/desktop/ui/style.css): ink #1b3035, teal #18625f, deep teal #174e4b, ground #f3f5f5, panel #ffffff, mint #e8f2ee, sage #93b9ac, muted #657775, error #974d39 on #fbece8. Dark scenes use ink ground with mint/sage text. No gradients, no emoji.
- Type: Source Serif 4 (titles), IBM Plex Sans (body, captions), IBM Plex Mono (evidence ids, numbers, hashes). Load from Google Fonts or vendor the WOFF2 files into the project; declare fallbacks.
- Safe zones: keep text inside 5 percent margins. One idea per scene. Captions never cover UI content.
- Music: none in the first cut (silence under narration is acceptable); a later cut may add a quiet bed from a licensed local library, never a web-hunted track.
- Narration: Spanish, neutral Latin American, calm clinical tone. Generated with ElevenLabs TTS (API key `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` in `~/.config/adwen/secrets.env`; read ONLY that key with `awk -F= '/^ELEVENLABS_API_KEY=/{...}'`, never source the file, never print the key). One MP3 per scene under `video/psyrec-demo/audio/`, timings measured with ffprobe and written to `audio/manifest.json`; scene durations derive from the audio, not the other way round.
- B-roll: three short ambient clips generated by the coordinator with Higgsfield (no faces, no real documents) will be dropped into `video/psyrec-demo/broll/` with a manifest; until they exist, scenes use styled placeholders (flat panels with the scene's diagram) and the build must still render.
- Screen captures: `video/psyrec-demo/captures/` receives real desktop and phone recordings later (placeholders now: a framed panel labelled "captura de pantalla pendiente" in the exact aspect of the target).

## Project layout to create (video/psyrec-demo)
- package.json (private, `hyperframes` pinned to 0.8.33, scripts: `dev`, `render`, `check`), node_modules ignored.
- composition source per the HyperFrames CLI conventions (one composition, nine scenes, GSAP timelines keyed to audio durations from audio/manifest.json).
- data/results.json schema: { generatedAt, source: [evidence paths], configurations: [{ label, model, quantization, reasoning, schema, strictGoldRecall, termOnlyRecall, forbiddenHits, firstPassValidity, abstentionCorrect, loadMs, nativeTtftMs, tokensPerSecond, promptTokens, generatedTokens }], hardware, disclosure, pending: boolean }.
- scripts: `scripts/tts.mjs` (ElevenLabs, writes audio + manifest), `scripts/check.mjs` (banned-hex grep against the palette list, safe-zone lint of scene text boxes, total duration <= 300 s, every on-screen number traced to results.json), `scripts/frames.mjs` (render, then extract a frame every 5 s to review/ for the self-review loop).
- README.md: how to regenerate audio, drop captures and B-roll, fill results.json, render.

## Standing rule (Jeff, 2026-09-10)
Generative clips (Higgsfield or any model) must contain no text at all: no letters, words, logos or readable screens. Every on-screen word is HyperFrames typography. The accepted clips in broll/manifest.json were selected under this rule; never use a rejected clip.
